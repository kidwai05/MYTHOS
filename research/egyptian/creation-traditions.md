# Egyptian creation traditions

Egyptian cosmology was not uniform, so each tradition is its own record. Deity records carry a `tradition` id (see `docs/database-schema.md`).

| Tradition id | Tradition | Important deities | Research focus | Key source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `heliopolitan` | Heliopolitan | Atum, Shu, Tefnut, Geb, Nut | Ennead and divine genealogy | _to be assigned_ |
| `hermopolitan` | Hermopolitan | Ogdoad | Primordial waters and creation | _to be assigned_ |
| `memphite` | Memphite | Ptah | Creation through thought and speech | [Memphite Theology](primary-sources/memphite-theology.md) |
| `theban` | Theban | Amun | Divine sovereignty and creative power | _to be assigned_ |
| `esna` | Esna | Khnum | Creation and formation of life | _to be assigned_ |
